# საძმაკაცოს რანკი — Build Plan

A weekly ranking app for a closed group of ~20 lifelong friends. Live scoreboard, weekly close, posts, comments, reactions, all-time stats. Georgian-only UI, mobile-first, dark by default.

**Stack:** Vite + React 18 + TypeScript · MUI v6 (+ MUI X Charts, MUI X Date Pickers) · Supabase (Postgres, Auth, Storage, Realtime, pg_cron) · TanStack Query · React Router v6 · Framer Motion · dayjs (`ka` locale) · Vercel

---

## 0. Rules for the implementer

Read these before writing code. They are the constraints that keep the app correct.

1. **Individual votes are secret from users, visible to admin.** No client query may ever be able to resolve *who* voted for whom. This is enforced in Postgres (RLS + aggregate-only views), never in the frontend. Getting this wrong breaks the whole social contract of the app.
2. **Live totals are public all week.** Aggregates (up count, down count, net) are visible to every member in real time. Only voter identity is hidden.
3. **Past weeks are immutable snapshots.** Closed weeks are read from `weekly_results`, never recomputed from `votes`.
4. **All user-facing strings are Georgian.** No English in the UI. Code, comments, table names, and variable names stay English.
5. **Mobile is the primary target.** Design and test at 390×844 first, then scale up. Every tap target ≥ 44px.
6. **No email sending, no email verification.** Google OAuth only.
7. Timezone is `Asia/Tbilisi` (UTC+4, no DST). Store everything as `timestamptz`, render in Tbilisi time.

## 1. Product spec

### 1.1 The week

- A week runs **Monday 00:00 → next Monday 00:00, Asia/Tbilisi**.
- Exactly one week has `status = 'open'` at a time.
- At close: the open week is snapshotted and frozen, and the next week opens in the same transaction. There is no gap — voting for the new week starts the instant the old one ends.
- A **countdown timer** to close is visible on the landing page at all times.
- Votes stay fully changeable until the buzzer. No freeze period.
- **Week 1 is extended.** The site goes live mid-week (Fri 14 Aug 2026); week 1 starts at launch and runs to **Mon 24 Aug 2026 00:00 Tbilisi**, skipping the immediate Monday. Seed this row manually with a custom `starts_at`; every subsequent week is generated by the cron function on the normal schedule.

### 1.2 Voting on people

- Each member may cast **one vote per other member per week**: `+1`, `-1`, or nothing.
- No self-voting (DB check constraint, not just UI).
- Votes are changeable and removable until close.
- Members added mid-week are immediately votable.

### 1.3 Scoring and ranking

For each member in a week:

```
up          = count(value = +1)
down        = count(value = -1)
net         = up - down
total_votes = up + down          -- engagement / polarization
```

**Sort order:** `net DESC, total_votes DESC, nickname ASC`

**Rank assignment:** competition ranking on the pair `(net, total_votes)`. Two members tied on both share a rank; the next rank skips accordingly → `1, 1, 3, 4, ...`. `nickname` only breaks display order within a shared rank, never the rank number itself.

This is what makes `+1/−1` (net 0, total 2) outrank `0/0` (net 0, total 0) — someone who provoked reactions places above someone nobody thought about.

**Rank movement** is computed against the previous *closed* week and displayed as `▲2 / ▼1 / –`. Members with no previous week show `NEW`.

### 1.4 Posts

- **One post per member per week.** 150 character limit. Text only.
- **One-shot:** once submitted it cannot be edited or deleted by the author. A confirmation dialog states this explicitly before submit. Admin can delete any post.
- Author is **visible** (nickname + avatar).
- Posts get **up/downvotes**, one per member per post, changeable. Voter identity is anonymous. **Self-voting on your own post is allowed.**
- Posts also get **reactions** (fixed emoji set).
- Post scores are entirely separate from person rankings — they never feed into the ranking.
- Landing page shows the **current week's** posts in a horizontally scrolling section, sorted by post net score descending. Past weeks' posts appear on that week's archive page.

### 1.5 Comments

- **One thread per week**, attached to the week (not to individual people).
- 100 character limit. Editable by the author. Deletable by the author or the admin (soft delete → `წაშლილია`).
- **Threads lock when the week closes.** Archive pages show them read-only.
- Comments do not get reactions.

### 1.6 Reactions

Fixed set: `🔥 😂 💀 👑 😭`

Placed on:
- **Ranking rows** (current week only, scoped to `week_id` + `member_id`)
- **Posts**

One of each emoji per member per target, toggleable. Anonymous — show counts only.

### 1.7 All-time

Two sortable columns, both shown:
- **Total net** — sum of `net` across all closed weeks
- **Average net per week** — fairer to members who joined later

Plus **weeks at #1** counter, and badges.

### 1.8 Badges

Awarded automatically at week close, stored in `member_badges`:

| key | Georgian label | condition |
|---|---|---|
| `weekly_king` | კვირის მეფე | rank 1 that week |
| `crown_streak_3` | სამი კვირა ტახტზე | rank 1 three consecutive weeks |
| `top_climber` | კვირის ამწევი | biggest positive rank movement |
| `top_faller` | კვირის ჩამვარდნილი | biggest negative rank movement |
| `most_hated` | კვირის ანტიგმირი | most downvotes that week |
| `polarizing` | გამყოფი | highest `total_votes` with `abs(net) <= 1` |
| `ghost` | აჩრდილი | zero votes received that week |
| `all_time_leader` | ლეგენდა | currently #1 all-time |

## 2. Data model

Single group, no `group_id` — the schema is deliberately hard-coded to one friend circle.

```sql
-- ============ MEMBERS ============
-- The ~20 rankable people. Created by admin BEFORE anyone signs in.
create table members (
  id            uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  nickname      text not null unique check (char_length(nickname) between 2 and 24),
  bio           text check (char_length(bio) <= 160),
  avatar_url    text,
  is_active     boolean not null default true,
  is_admin      boolean not null default false,
  auth_user_id  uuid unique references auth.users(id) on delete set null,
  created_at    timestamptz not null default now()
);
create unique index members_single_admin on members (is_admin) where is_admin;

-- ============ PENDING ACCOUNTS ============
-- Every Google sign-in lands here until admin links it to a member.
create table pending_accounts (
  auth_user_id  uuid primary key references auth.users(id) on delete cascade,
  email         text not null,
  google_name   text,
  google_avatar text,
  created_at    timestamptz not null default now()
);

-- ============ WEEKS ============
create table weeks (
  id          int primary key generated always as identity,
  starts_at   timestamptz not null,
  ends_at     timestamptz not null,
  status      text not null default 'open' check (status in ('open','closed')),
  closed_at   timestamptz,
  check (ends_at > starts_at)
);
create unique index weeks_one_open on weeks (status) where status = 'open';

-- ============ VOTES (secret) ============
create table votes (
  id          uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  week_id     int not null references weeks(id) on delete cascade,
  voter_id    uuid not null references members(id) on delete cascade,
  target_id   uuid not null references members(id) on delete cascade,
  value       smallint not null check (value in (-1, 1)),
  created_at  timestamptz not null default now(),
  updated_at  timestamptz not null default now(),
  unique (week_id, voter_id, target_id),
  check (voter_id <> target_id)
);
create index votes_week_target on votes (week_id, target_id);

-- ============ VOTE EVENTS (public, identity-free) ============
-- Realtime fan-out channel. Contains NO voter identity.
create table vote_events (
  id         bigint primary key generated always as identity,
  week_id    int not null,
  target_id  uuid not null,
  created_at timestamptz not null default now()
);

-- ============ POSTS ============
create table posts (
  id         uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  week_id    int not null references weeks(id) on delete cascade,
  author_id  uuid not null references members(id) on delete cascade,
  body       text not null check (char_length(body) between 1 and 150),
  created_at timestamptz not null default now(),
  unique (week_id, author_id)
);

create table post_votes (
  post_id    uuid not null references posts(id) on delete cascade,
  voter_id   uuid not null references members(id) on delete cascade,
  value      smallint not null check (value in (-1, 1)),
  updated_at timestamptz not null default now(),
  primary key (post_id, voter_id)
);
-- self-voting intentionally allowed

-- ============ COMMENTS ============
create table comments (
  id         uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  week_id    int not null references weeks(id) on delete cascade,
  author_id  uuid not null references members(id) on delete cascade,
  body       text not null check (char_length(body) between 1 and 100),
  created_at timestamptz not null default now(),
  updated_at timestamptz not null default now(),
  deleted_at timestamptz
);
create index comments_week on comments (week_id, created_at);

-- ============ REACTIONS ============
create table member_reactions (
  week_id    int not null references weeks(id) on delete cascade,
  member_id  uuid not null references members(id) on delete cascade,
  reactor_id uuid not null references members(id) on delete cascade,
  emoji      text not null check (emoji in ('🔥','😂','💀','👑','😭')),
  primary key (week_id, member_id, reactor_id, emoji)
);

create table post_reactions (
  post_id    uuid not null references posts(id) on delete cascade,
  reactor_id uuid not null references members(id) on delete cascade,
  emoji      text not null check (emoji in ('🔥','😂','💀','👑','😭')),
  primary key (post_id, reactor_id, emoji)
);

-- ============ SNAPSHOTS ============
create table weekly_results (
  week_id     int not null references weeks(id) on delete cascade,
  member_id   uuid not null references members(id) on delete cascade,
  up          int not null,
  down        int not null,
  net         int not null,
  total_votes int not null,
  rank        int not null,
  prev_rank   int,
  movement    int,          -- prev_rank - rank; null when NEW
  primary key (week_id, member_id)
);

create table member_badges (
  id         uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  member_id  uuid not null references members(id) on delete cascade,
  badge_key  text not null,
  week_id    int references weeks(id) on delete cascade,
  awarded_at timestamptz not null default now(),
  unique (member_id, badge_key, week_id)
);

-- ============ ADMIN ============
create table announcements (
  id         uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  body       text not null check (char_length(body) <= 280),
  is_active  boolean not null default true,
  created_at timestamptz not null default now()
);

create table audit_log (
  id         bigint primary key generated always as identity,
  actor_id   uuid references members(id) on delete set null,
  action     text not null,
  detail     jsonb,
  created_at timestamptz not null default now()
);
```

### 2.1 Helper functions

```sql
-- Current member for the logged-in user
create or replace function current_member_id() returns uuid
language sql stable security definer set search_path = public as $$
  select id from members where auth_user_id = auth.uid() and is_active
$$;

create or replace function is_admin() returns boolean
language sql stable security definer set search_path = public as $$
  select coalesce((select is_admin from members where auth_user_id = auth.uid()), false)
$$;
```

### 2.2 Aggregate view (the anonymity boundary)

```sql
-- SECURITY DEFINER view: exposes counts, never voter_id.
create view live_standings
with (security_invoker = false) as
select
  w.id as week_id,
  m.id as member_id,
  m.nickname,
  m.avatar_url,
  count(*) filter (where v.value = 1)  as up,
  count(*) filter (where v.value = -1) as down,
  coalesce(sum(v.value), 0)            as net,
  count(v.id)                          as total_votes
from weeks w
cross join members m
left join votes v on v.week_id = w.id and v.target_id = m.id
where w.status = 'open' and m.is_active
group by w.id, m.id;

grant select on live_standings to authenticated;
```

Ranking is computed **client-side** from this view (trivial for 20 rows) so ties and movement render consistently with the snapshot logic. Movement on the live board compares against the most recent closed week.

Do the same for post scores:

```sql
create view post_scores with (security_invoker = false) as
select p.id as post_id,
       count(*) filter (where pv.value = 1)  as up,
       count(*) filter (where pv.value = -1) as down,
       coalesce(sum(pv.value), 0)            as net
from posts p left join post_votes pv on pv.post_id = p.id
group by p.id;
grant select on post_scores to authenticated;
```

## 3. RLS policies

Enable RLS on **every** table. Key policies:

| Table | authenticated member | admin |
|---|---|---|
| `members` | `select` all active; `update` own row (nickname, bio, avatar only) | full |
| `pending_accounts` | `select` own row only | full |
| `weeks` | `select` all | full |
| `votes` | `select` **own rows only**; `insert/update/delete` own rows where `week.status='open'` and `voter_id = current_member_id()` | `select` all |
| `vote_events` | `select` all | full |
| `posts` | `select` all; `insert` own, once per open week; no update/delete | full |
| `post_votes` | `select` own only; upsert/delete own | `select` all |
| `comments` | `select` all; `insert` own on open week; `update` own on open week; soft-delete own | full |
| `member_reactions` / `post_reactions` | `select` all; insert/delete own | full |
| `weekly_results`, `member_badges` | `select` all | full |
| `announcements` | `select` where `is_active` | full |
| `audit_log` | none | `select` all |

Aggregates reach the client **only** through `live_standings` and `post_scores`. Reaction counts are safe to read directly since reactions are not secret in the same way — but still render counts only, never `reactor_id`, and expose a separate `my_reactions` query filtered to `current_member_id()`.

**Verification step (do not skip):** after writing policies, log in as a non-admin and attempt `select * from votes` — it must return only that member's own rows. Write this as a test in `supabase/tests/anonymity.sql`.

## 4. Week close job

```sql
create or replace function close_current_week() returns void
language plpgsql security definer set search_path = public as $$
declare
  w_id int; w_end timestamptz; prev_week int;
begin
  select id, ends_at into w_id, w_end from weeks where status='open' for update;
  if w_id is null then return; end if;

  select id into prev_week from weeks
   where status='closed' and id <> w_id order by id desc limit 1;

  -- 1. snapshot with competition ranking
  insert into weekly_results (week_id, member_id, up, down, net, total_votes, rank, prev_rank, movement)
  with agg as (
    select m.id as member_id,
           count(*) filter (where v.value=1)  as up,
           count(*) filter (where v.value=-1) as down,
           coalesce(sum(v.value),0)           as net,
           count(v.id)                        as total_votes
    from members m
    left join votes v on v.week_id=w_id and v.target_id=m.id
    where m.is_active
    group by m.id
  ), ranked as (
    select a.*, rank() over (order by a.net desc, a.total_votes desc) as rank
    from agg a
  )
  select r.member_id, r.up, r.down, r.net, r.total_votes, r.rank,
         pr.rank, pr.rank - r.rank
  from ranked r
  left join weekly_results pr on pr.week_id = prev_week and pr.member_id = r.member_id;

  -- 2. badges (weekly_king, top_climber, top_faller, most_hated, polarizing,
  --    ghost, crown_streak_3, all_time_leader)  -- see §1.8

  -- 3. freeze
  update weeks set status='closed', closed_at=now() where id=w_id;

  -- 4. open the next week, back-to-back
  insert into weeks (starts_at, ends_at, status)
  values (w_end, w_end + interval '7 days', 'open');

  insert into audit_log (action, detail) values ('week_closed', jsonb_build_object('week_id', w_id));
end $$;
```

**Schedule.** Tbilisi is UTC+4 year-round, so Monday 00:00 Tbilisi = **Sunday 20:00 UTC**:

```sql
select cron.schedule('close-week', '0 20 * * 0', $$ select close_current_week(); $$);
```

Make `close_current_week()` idempotent-safe by keying off `status='open'` with `for update`, and have it no-op if `now() < ends_at - interval '1 minute'` unless called with a `force` flag (admin manual close).

**Seed week 1 manually:**
```sql
insert into weeks (starts_at, ends_at, status)
values (now(), timestamptz '2026-08-24 00:00+04', 'open');
```

## 5. Realtime

Users cannot `select` other people's votes, so they will never receive Realtime events from the `votes` table. That's the point — and it's why `vote_events` exists.

```sql
create or replace function emit_vote_event() returns trigger
language plpgsql security definer as $$
begin
  insert into vote_events (week_id, target_id)
  values (coalesce(new.week_id, old.week_id), coalesce(new.target_id, old.target_id));
  return null;
end $$;

create trigger votes_emit after insert or update or delete on votes
for each row execute function emit_vote_event();
```

Client subscribes to `vote_events` inserts and to `posts`, `post_votes` (via a similar event table or by polling `post_scores`), `comments`, and reaction tables. On any `vote_events` insert → invalidate the `live_standings` query in TanStack Query. Debounce invalidation to ~400ms so a burst of votes causes one refetch.

Add a **turnout indicator**: `14/20 ხმა მიცემულია` — count of distinct voters in the open week, from a second aggregate view.

Prune `vote_events` older than 7 days with a nightly cron job.

## 6. Storage

Bucket `avatars`, public read.

- Path convention: `avatars/{member_id}.{ext}`
- Upload policy: `authenticated` may write where the path's member_id equals `current_member_id()`; admin may write any path.
- Client-side: resize/crop to 512×512 square and compress to WebP before upload (use `browser-image-compression` or a canvas util). Max 2MB pre-compression.
- Fallback avatar: MUI `<Avatar>` with the first Georgian letter of the nickname on an ember-tinted background derived from a hash of `member_id`.

## 7. Auth & onboarding

Google OAuth only. No email verification, no password flow.

**Flow:**
1. Admin pre-creates all ~20 members in `/admin/members` (nickname + optional avatar). They exist with `auth_user_id = null`.
2. A friend hits the site → `შესვლა Google-ით`.
3. A trigger on `auth.users` insert writes a row into `pending_accounts` (email, name, Google avatar).
4. If the signed-in user has no linked member → route to `/pending`, a full-screen waiting state. No app data is reachable.
5. Admin sees pending accounts in `/admin/accounts` and links each to a pre-created member (a dropdown of unlinked members). Linking sets `members.auth_user_id` and deletes the pending row.
6. On next load the user is in.

```sql
create or replace function handle_new_auth_user() returns trigger
language plpgsql security definer set search_path = public as $$
begin
  if not exists (select 1 from members where auth_user_id = new.id) then
    insert into pending_accounts (auth_user_id, email, google_name, google_avatar)
    values (new.id, new.email,
            new.raw_user_meta_data->>'full_name',
            new.raw_user_meta_data->>'avatar_url')
    on conflict do nothing;
  end if;
  return new;
end $$;

create trigger on_auth_user_created after insert on auth.users
for each row execute function handle_new_auth_user();
```

**Admin bootstrap:** after your own Google account is created, run once in the SQL editor:
```sql
update members set is_admin = true, auth_user_id = '<your-auth-uid>' where nickname = '<your-nickname>';
```
There is exactly one admin (enforced by the partial unique index). The admin is a normal player on the public site — same profile, same votes received — with `/admin` as the only difference.

**Route guards:**
- Not signed in → `/login`
- Signed in, no member → `/pending`
- Signed in, member, `is_admin = false`, requesting `/admin/*` → 404 (not a 403 — don't advertise it)

## 9. Design system

### 9.1 Direction

The brief pins dark + `#F73718` + Apple-clean components + Spotify-Wrapped energy. The risk with "dark background, one hot accent" is that it lands on the generic AI-app default. Three choices pull it away from that:

1. **The dark is warm, not neutral.** Backgrounds carry a red-brown tint (`#14100F`, not `#0A0A0A`), so the surface feels like cooling charcoal rather than a generic void.
2. **Downvotes are cold, not red.** Up = ember amber, down = a desaturated cool slate-blue. The scoreboard becomes a temperature scale — warm rising, cold sinking — instead of a red/green traffic light. `#F73718` stays reserved for brand and interactive accents only, so it never gets diluted.
3. **Wrapped energy is quarantined.** Big blocks, oversized numerals, and gradients appear only on profile pages, week summaries, and badge reveals. The main scoreboard stays restrained and Apple-ish: generous whitespace, hairline dividers, subtle depth.

Radii: `8 / 12 / 16 / 999`. Elevation via layered surfaces + 1px borders, not heavy shadows. Disable MUI's default gradient overlays (`MuiPaper: { defaultProps: { elevation: 0 } }`).

### 9.3 Typography

- **Display / headings / numerals — FiraGO**, weights 600–800. Full Georgian coverage with more character than the system default. Self-host via `@fontsource/firago` or a local `woff2` in `/public/fonts` (do **not** rely on Google Fonts — FiraGO is not hosted there).
- **Body / UI — Noto Sans Georgian**, variable, weights 400–700, from Google Fonts.
- Fallback stack: `'FiraGO', 'Noto Sans Georgian', 'BPG Arial', system-ui, sans-serif`.

Georgian has no uppercase — never use `text-transform: uppercase`, and don't lean on caps for hierarchy. Build hierarchy from **weight, size, and color** instead. Georgian also runs visually denser than Latin: set `line-height: 1.55` on body text and add `letter-spacing: 0.01em` at small sizes.

Scale: `display 44/700 · h1 32/700 · h2 24/600 · h3 19/600 · body 15/400 · caption 13/400`. Rank numerals: FiraGO 800, `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`, tight tracking (`-0.03em`).

### 9.4 Signature element — the HeatBar

Each standings row carries a **diverging bar from a center axis**: upvotes extend right in ember amber, downvotes extend left in cool slate, both scaled against the week's maximum `total_votes`. A person at +8/−1 shows a long warm bar. A person at +7/−7 shows a wide bar in both directions — instantly readable as *divisive*, which a net score alone would completely hide. Someone with no votes shows only the bare center axis.

This is the one memorable thing in the app. Everything around it stays quiet.

### 9.5 Motion

Framer Motion, kept restrained:

- `<Reorder>` / `layoutId` on standings rows so positions **slide** when a vote lands — the single most satisfying moment in the app.
- Vote button: 120ms spring scale + a brief HeatBar glow.
- Route transitions: 180ms fade + 8px rise.
- Countdown: subtle pulse when under 1 hour.
- Week close / badge reveal: one staged Wrapped-style sequence (numbers count up, badges stagger in) — **this is the only place elaborate animation is allowed.**
- Wrap everything in `useReducedMotion()`.

### 9.6 Mobile

Bottom navigation bar (რანკი · პოსტები · არქივი · პროფილი). Standings rows collapse to: rank · avatar · nickname · HeatBar · vote buttons, with `up/down` counts on a second line. Posts scroller uses CSS scroll-snap with a partial next-card peek so the horizontal scroll is discoverable. Test at 390×844 before anything else.

## 11. Admin panel

`/admin`, single admin, hidden route (404 for everyone else). Every mutating action writes to `audit_log`.

- **Dashboard** — turnout this week, votes cast, posts, comments, time to close.
- **Accounts** — pending Google sign-ins; link each to an unlinked member; reject.
- **Members** — create, edit nickname/bio/avatar (override anyone's), deactivate (removed from voting, history preserved), reactivate.
- **Vote matrix** — 20×20 grid for any week showing exactly who voted for whom. Admin-only.
- **Week controls** — extend `ends_at`, pause voting, force-close now, adjust the next week's start.
- **Moderation** — delete any post or comment, void an individual vote.
- **Results** — edit a past `weekly_results` row; the edit is logged and marked.
- **Announcements** — a banner on the landing page.
- **Audit log** — full history, read-only.

## 12. Seed data

`supabase/seed.sql`, dev only, guarded so it can't run against production:

- 20 members with Georgian nicknames and generated avatars (DiceBear or solid ember-tinted initials).
- 6 closed weeks with plausible vote distributions — include a tie at #1, a member with zero votes, and a polarizing +7/−7 case so the HeatBar and tie logic are visibly exercised.
- Posts on each of those weeks with varied scores, plus comment threads and reactions.
- Badges awarded by running `close_current_week()` over the seeded weeks rather than inserting badge rows by hand — this also tests the close function.

## 13. Build order

Each phase ends at a checkpoint you can look at and verify. Don't start the next phase until the checkpoint passes.

**Phase 1 — Foundation**
Supabase project, full schema, RLS, helper functions, views, triggers, seed data. Vite + TS + MUI scaffold, theme file, fonts, `ka.ts`. Google OAuth configured.
*Checkpoint:* seed data visible via a raw table dump; the anonymity test passes.

**Phase 2 — Auth & shell**
Login, pending screen, route guards, admin bootstrap, app shell with bottom nav, dark/light toggle persisted to `localStorage`.
*Checkpoint:* you sign in, land on `/pending`, self-link via SQL, and reach the empty landing page.

**Phase 3 — Voting core**
`live_standings`, StandingsList, StandingsRow, HeatBar, VoteToggle, `cast_vote` RPC, optimistic updates, countdown, turnout, rank deltas.
*Checkpoint:* two browser sessions, two accounts, votes visible to both — and neither can see who cast what.

**Phase 4 — Realtime & motion**
`vote_events` subscription, debounced invalidation, Framer layout reordering.
*Checkpoint:* vote in one window, rows visibly reorder in the other within ~1s.

**Phase 5 — Week lifecycle**
`close_current_week()`, badge logic, pg_cron, archive index, week picker, `/weeks/:id`.
*Checkpoint:* manually invoke close — snapshot correct, next week open, comments locked, badges awarded.

**Phase 6 — Posts, comments, reactions**
PostComposer with the one-shot dialog, PostScroller, post voting, comment thread, ReactionBar in both places.
*Checkpoint:* full loop works on a 390px viewport.

**Phase 7 — Profiles & all-time**
Rank history chart, badge shelf, week breakdown, all-time table with both columns, `/me` editing, avatar upload.
*Checkpoint:* six seeded weeks produce a sensible chart and all-time table.

**Phase 8 — Admin & polish**
All admin routes, audit log, announcements. Then: mobile pass, reduced-motion pass, keyboard focus states, loading skeletons, error boundaries, Georgian copy review, Lighthouse.
*Checkpoint:* deployed to Vercel, tested on a real phone.

## 15. Non-goals for v1

Explicitly out of scope — do not build these: email or push notifications, categories or multiple ranking dimensions, images in posts, multiple friend groups, multiple admins, vote reasons, seasons/resets, comments on closed weeks, editing or deleting your own post, public sharing of results outside the group.
